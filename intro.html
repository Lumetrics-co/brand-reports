AI Script Generation
Overall Strategy
Combine expert authority with clear, visual explanations, addressing common pain points and debunking myths, culminating in actionable advice and calls to action.
Script Structure
Hook: Present a common question or misconception
Introduction & Credibility: Introduce the expert and the video's purpose
Problem Elaboration: Explain the underlying issue or risk clearly, using visuals
Visual Aid / Example: Show a product or a visual explaining the issue.
Debunking Misconceptions: Address popular myths or misleading marketing terms
Actionable Solutions: Provide specific recommendations or criteria for safe choices
Summary & Key Takeaways: Recap the most important points
Call to Action: Encourage engagement and further learning
Visual Cues
Talking head for expert delivery.
Product shots/illustrations for examples.
Animated text overlays for key terms, definitions, and warnings
Simple graphics/animations to illustrate concepts.

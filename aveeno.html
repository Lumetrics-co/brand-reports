Executive Summary · Aveeno Baby Video Strategy

The Verdict: Elite Content, Inefficient Machine

★
Strength
When it lands, nothing else comes close — on either platform
  • YouTube: top videos earn 11,379 avg. engagement on 1M+ view content — 7x–1,527x every competitor
  • YouTube: Share of Mind holds at 72.6% despite Share of Voice of just 3.8%; audience sentiment skews positive (68.3% of comments)
  • Instagram: Mind Share holds at 24–26% of category engagement from just ~2% of Voice Share (+23–24pp divergence, 2nd-best of 5 brands); 79% of comments are positive
!
Risk
YouTube's engine is leaking budget; Instagram needs on-platform polish
  • YouTube Channel Health Score: 38/100 — zero videos rated "Good"; ₹1,12,000 spent on 14 videos stuck under 10,000 views (₹1.93/view); 100% of Mar–Aug videos fell under 0.5% engagement
  • Instagram engagement stayed healthy (0 posts under 0.5%), but 9 of 14 Mar–Aug posts (64%) score "Poor" for on-platform optimization — a captions/hashtags/CTA gap, not a content-quality gap
→
Opportunity
Two proven formulas, ready to apply
  • YouTube: 5 of 11 parameters show a clear winner (>20% lead) — Mixed-gender cast, 3D CGI animation, Before-After-Bridge story, Evergreen topics, Gen X talent
  • Instagram: 10 of 11 parameters show a clear winner (>20% lead) — Product Demo format, Problem-Solution narrative, Tracking camera movement, Mixed-gender cast, Kinetic Typography animation (+26–322% lift)
  • Applying both turns isolated wins into a repeatable, cross-platform content system
Aveeno Baby doesn't have a content problem on either platform — it has a consistency and optimization problem. The best videos and posts already beat every competitor; fix YouTube's SEO and budget leaks, tighten Instagram's on-platform basics, and apply the proven formula on each channel to scale what's already working.

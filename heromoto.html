Executive Summary · Hero MotoCorp Video Strategy

The Verdict: Real Goodwill, Nearly Invisible Machine

★
Strength
Genuine audience affection exists, even without reach
  • Comment sentiment runs 54.6% positive lifetime (15,462 comments analysed) vs. 35.6% negative — nostalgia for legacy campaigns (Splendor+, Glamour X) still drives real, unprompted engagement
  • 748 lifetime videos published — more raw content volume than TVS (247) or Bajaj (46), a latent library to re-optimize rather than rebuild from zero
!
Risk
Honda owns the category on every axis that matters
  • Share of Voice 0.12% and Share of Mind 0.37% — Honda holds 77.0% / 84.7%; even Bajaj (15.3%/4.9%) and TVS (7.6%/10.1%) lead Hero
  • Channel Health Score 15/100 — 744 of 748 videos (99.5%) rated "Critical"; only 1 video has ever crossed 1M views, earning 11.6× less engagement than Honda's 1M+ average
  • Per-video waste analysis is currently blocked by a Lumetrics data-sync gap on this channel — flagged for follow-up, not swept under the rug
→
Opportunity
Fix the machine before writing new content
  • A 15/100 health score with 54.6% positive sentiment means the SEO/discoverability layer, not the audience's opinion of the brand, is the bottleneck — the cheaper problem to fix
  • Content Deepdive parameter-winner analysis is pending a plan upgrade; once unlocked, it will show exactly which formats and hooks in the existing 748-video library deserve re-promotion
  • Closing even a fraction of the Voice/Mind gap to Honda is achievable by re-optimizing existing videos before investing in net-new production
Hero MotoCorp doesn't have an audience-goodwill problem — sentiment is genuinely positive. It has a discoverability and optimization problem: a 748-video library sitting at a 15/100 health score, invisible next to Honda's dominant Voice and Mind Share.

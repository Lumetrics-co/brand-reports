Opportunity
A proven formula, ready to apply — with less dependency
  • 8 of 11 content parameters show a statistically clear winner (>20% lead) at N=21
  • Proven formula: Evergreen lifespan, Problem Statement hook, Dolly Out movement, Motion Graphics animation, Data/Statistics trust signals

Denver doesn't have a content-quality problem — its best work already rivals the category leader on a fraction of the audience. It has a consistency and dependency problem: reach collapsed the moment paid boosts and celebrity buzz cooled.
